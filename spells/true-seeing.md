# True Seeing
*Level 6 Divination (Bard, Cleric, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard) | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (mushroom powder worth 25+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Classes:** [[classes/bard|Bard]], [[classes/cleric|Cleric]], [[classes/sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[classes/warlock|Warlock]], [[classes/wizard|Wizard]]

For the duration, the willing creature you touch has Truesight with a range of 120 feet.
