# Darkvision
*Level 2 Transmutation (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard) | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a dried carrot)
- **Duration:** 8 hour
- **Classes:** [[classes/druid|Druid]], [[classes/ranger|Ranger]], [[classes/sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[classes/wizard|Wizard]]

For the duration, a willing creature you touch has Darkvision with a range of 150 feet.
