# Ember Ward

*Level 1 Abjuration*

A ring of low embers circles you for the duration. A creature that ends its
turn within 5 feet of you takes 1d6 Fire damage.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell
slot level above 1.
