# Power Word Kill
*Level 9 Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard) | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Classes:** [[classes/bard|Bard]], [[classes/sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[classes/warlock|Warlock]], [[classes/wizard|Wizard]]

You compel one creature you can see within range to die. If the target has 100 Hit Points or fewer, it dies. Otherwise, it takes 12d12 Psychic damage.
