# Protection from Energy
*Level 3 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard) | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
- **Classes:** [[classes/cleric|Cleric]], [[classes/druid|Druid]], [[classes/ranger|Ranger]], [[classes/sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[classes/wizard|Wizard]]

For the duration, the willing creature you touch has Resistance to one damage type of your choice: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder.
