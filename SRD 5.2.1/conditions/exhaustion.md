# Exhaustion
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> Exhaustion is a unified 6-level track where each level imposes a cumulative -2 penalty to all d20 tests and reduces speed by 5 feet. Level 6 is death.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Exhaustion condition, you experience the following effects.

#### Exhaustion Levels

This condition is cumulative. Each time you receive it, you gain 1 Exhaustion level. You die if your Exhaustion level is 6.

#### D20 Tests Affected

When you make a D20 Test, the roll is reduced by 2 times your Exhaustion level.

#### Speed Reduced

Your Speed is reduced by a number of feet equal to 5 times your Exhaustion level.

#### Removing Exhaustion Levels

Finishing a Long Rest removes 1 of your Exhaustion levels. When your Exhaustion level reaches 0, the condition ends.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Cumulative Levels:** Exhaustion is measured in six levels. An effect can give you one or more levels of exhaustion.
- **D20 Tests Penalty:** For each level of exhaustion you have, subtract 2 from all your d20 Tests (attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws).
- **Speed Reduction:** For each level of exhaustion, reduce your Speed by 5 feet.
- **Death at Level 6:** If your exhaustion level reaches 6, you immediately die.
- **Removing Exhaustion:** Finishing a Long Rest removes 1 of your exhaustion levels, provided you have also ingested food and drink.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- **Deadly Death Spiral:** Taking even 1 level of exhaustion makes spellcasters significantly more vulnerable to losing Concentration, as DC 10 Concentration checks are effectively made with a $-2$ penalty.
- **Death Saves:** Death saving throws are d20 tests! A character with Exhaustion suffers $-2$ per level to their death saving throws, dramatically increasing lethality.

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- When running environmental hazards (extreme heat, cold, forced march), these rules are much faster to calculate on the fly without looking up the table.
- A creature with 30 ft speed reaches 0 speed at 6 levels anyway, aligning with death.
