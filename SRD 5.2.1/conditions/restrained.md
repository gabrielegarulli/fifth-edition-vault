# Restrained
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> A restrained creature has 0 speed, gives advantage to attackers, attacks with disadvantage, has disadvantage on Dex saves, and has disadvantage attacking non-restrainers.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Restrained condition, you experience the following effects.

#### Speed 0

Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.

#### Attacks Affected

Attack rolls against you have Advantage, and your attack rolls have Disadvantage.

#### Saving Throws Affected

You have Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Speed 0:** You can't benefit from any bonus to your speed.
- **Attacks Affected:** Attack rolls against you have Advantage, and your attack rolls have Disadvantage.
- **Dexterity Saves:** You have Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.
- **Attacks Against Others:** If you are restrained by a creature, you have Disadvantage on attack rolls against any target other than that creature.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- **AoE Spell Setup:** Restrained creatures are prime targets for *Fireball*, *Lightning Bolt*, or *Web* since their Dex saves have Disadvantage.
- **Critical Attack Combos:** Rogues gain automatic Sneak Attack against restrained targets due to Advantage.

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- Restrained is strictly more punitive than Grappled because it grants incoming attackers Advantage and penalizes Dexterity saving throws.
