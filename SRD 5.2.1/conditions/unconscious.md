# Unconscious
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> Incapacitated, Prone, drops what it is holding, unaware, auto-fails Str and Dex saves, attacks have advantage, hits within 5 ft are critical hits.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Unconscious condition, you experience the following effects.

#### Inert

You have the Incapacitated and Prone conditions, and you drop whatever you're holding. When this condition ends, you remain Prone.

#### Speed 0

Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.

#### Attacks Affected

Attack rolls against you have Advantage.

#### Saving Throws Affected

You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.

#### Automatic Critical Hits

Any attack roll that hits you is a Critical Hit if the attacker is within 5 feet of you.

#### Unaware

You're unaware of your surroundings.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Incapacitated & Prone:** You are Incapacitated and Prone.
- **Drops Items:** You drop whatever you are holding.
- **Unaware:** You are unaware of your surroundings.
- **Auto-Fail Saves:** You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
- **Attacks Against:** Attack rolls against you have Advantage.
- **Automatic Critical Hits:** Any attack roll that hits you is a Critical Hit if the attacker is within 5 feet of you.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- Taking damage while unconscious results in automatic death saving throw failures (2 failures if hit within 5 ft due to crit).

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- Healing restores consciousness immediately.
