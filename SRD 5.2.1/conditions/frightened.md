# Frightened
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while source of fear is in line of sight. Can't willingly move closer to the source.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Frightened condition, you experience the following effects.

#### Ability Checks and Attacks Affected

You have Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while the source of fear is within line of sight.

#### Can't Approach

You can't willingly move closer to the source of fear.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Disadvantage:** You have Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while the source of fear is within your line of sight.
- **Can't Approach:** You can't willingly move to a space that is closer to the source of fear than your current space.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- Prevents melee enemies from advancing onto squishy allies.

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- Breaking line of sight (ducking behind full cover) temporarily suspends the disadvantage.
