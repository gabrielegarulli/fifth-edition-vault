# Blinded
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> A blinded creature can't see and automatically fails checks requiring sight. Attack rolls against have advantage, its attacks have disadvantage.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Blinded condition, you experience the following effects.

#### Can't See

You can't see and automatically fail any ability check that requires sight.

#### Attacks Affected

Attack rolls against you have Advantage, and your attack rolls have Disadvantage.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Can't See:** You can't see and automatically fail any ability check that requires sight.
- **Attacks Affected:** Attack rolls against you have Advantage, and your attack rolls have Disadvantage.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- Shuts down spells that require seeing the target. Gives all allies advantage on melee and ranged attacks against the target.

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- If both combatants are blinded (e.g. in magical darkness without Darkvision), Advantage and Disadvantage cancel out to a normal roll.
