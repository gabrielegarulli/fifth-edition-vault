# Grappled
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> A grappled creature has 0 speed, suffers disadvantage attacking anyone other than the grappler, and gets a free saving throw to escape at the end of each of its turns.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Grappled condition, you experience the following effects.

#### Speed 0

Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.

#### Attacks Affected

You have Disadvantage on attack rolls against any target other than the grappler.

#### Movable

The grappler can drag or carry you when it moves, but every foot of movement costs it 1 extra foot unless you are Tiny or two or more sizes smaller than it.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Speed 0:** You can't benefit from any bonus to your speed.
- **Disadvantage Against Others (Soft Taunt):** You have Disadvantage on attack rolls against any target other than the grappler.
- **Movable:** The grappler can drag or carry you when it moves, but every foot of movement costs 1 extra foot (half speed), unless you are Tiny or two or more sizes smaller.
- **End-of-Turn Escape (No Action Required):** At the end of each of your turns, you can make a Strength or Dexterity saving throw (your choice) against the grapple escape DC (8 + Grappler's Str mod + PB, or spell save DC) to break free.
- **Condition Ends:** The condition also ends if the grappler becomes Incapacitated or if an effect moves you beyond the grapple's reach without using your speed.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- **Tanking Utility:** Frontline martial characters can grab dangerous enemy bruisers or skirmishers to protect their fragile spellcasters.
- **Hazard Dragging:** Grappling an enemy and dragging them across *Spike Growth* or into *Spirit Guardians* deals massive continuous damage.

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- Remember that forced movement (e.g. *Thunderwave*, *Repelling Blast*, or a shove) automatically breaks a grapple without requiring an escape check.
