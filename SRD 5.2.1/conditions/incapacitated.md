# Incapacitated
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> An incapacitated creature can't take actions, bonus actions, or reactions, immediately loses concentration, cannot speak, and has disadvantage on initiative.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Incapacitated condition, you experience the following effects.

#### Inactive

You can't take any action, Bonus Action, or Reaction.

#### No Concentration

Your Concentration is broken.

#### Speechless

You can't speak.

#### Surprised

If you're Incapacitated when you roll Initiative, you have Disadvantage on the roll.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Inactive:** You can't take any actions, bonus actions, or reactions.
- **Concentration Broken:** Your Concentration is immediately broken.
- **Speechless:** You can't speak.
- **Surprised Initiative Penalty:** If you are Incapacitated when you roll Initiative, you have Disadvantage on the roll.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- **Spell Canceler:** Inflicting Incapacitated instantly ends ongoing concentration spells like *Banishment*, *Spirit Guardians*, *Hypnotic Pattern*, or *Haste* (which triggers lethal Haste lethargy).

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- Many other conditions include Incapacitated as a component (Paralyzed, Petrified, Stunned, Unconscious).
