# Cloak of Elvenkind

*uncommon, requires attunement | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

While you wear this cloak, Wisdom (Perception) checks made to perceive you have Disadvantage, and you have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

```vault-magic-item
effects:
  - type: ability_check_advantage
    ability: dex
    skill: stealth
    trigger: with its hood up
```
