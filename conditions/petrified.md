# Petrified
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> Transformed into solid inanimate substance, Incapacitated, weight multiplied by 10, attacks have advantage, auto-fails Str/Dex saves, resistance to all damage, immune to poison/disease.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Petrified condition, you experience the following effects.

#### Turned to Inanimate Substance

You are transformed, along with any nonmagical objects you are wearing and carrying, into a solid inanimate substance (usually stone). Your weight increases by a factor of ten, and you cease aging.

#### Incapacitated

You have the Incapacitated condition.

#### Speed 0

Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.

#### Attacks Affected

Attack rolls against you have Advantage.

#### Saving Throws Affected

You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.

#### Resist Damage

You have Resistance to all damage.

#### Poison Immunity

You have Immunity to the Poisoned condition.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Turned to Stone:** You and any mundane item you carry are transformed into an inanimate stone substance.
- **Weight:** Your weight increases by a factor of 10, and you cease aging.
- **Incapacitated:** You are Incapacitated, can't move or speak, and are unaware of your surroundings.
- **Attacks & Saves:** Attack rolls against you have Advantage, and you automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saves.
- **Resistances & Immunities:** You have Resistance to all damage and are Immune to Poison and Disease.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- Requires Greater Restoration or Wish to undo.

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- Damage resistance applies before damage is dealt to stone form.
