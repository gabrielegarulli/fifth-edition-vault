# Bloodied
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> A creature is Bloodied whenever its current Hit Points are at or below half its maximum Hit Points.

## 📜 Rule Text

A creature is Bloodied while it has half its Hit Points or fewer remaining.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Threshold (≤ 50% HP):** A creature becomes Bloodied the moment its Hit Points drop to or below half its Hit Point maximum.
- **Visible Distress:** Being Bloodied is an observable physical state in combat; characters and monsters can tell when a creature is Bloodied without an ability check.
- **Triggering Effects:** Numerous monster attacks, legendary reactions, dragon breath recharges, and class abilities activate specifically when a creature or target is Bloodied.
- **Ending Bloodied:** Regaining Hit Points above 50% immediately removes the Bloodied state.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- **Monster Enrage Thresholds:** Many apex predators and boss monsters unlock high-damage bonus actions or immediate reaction attacks upon becoming Bloodied.
- **Target Prioritization:** Focus attacks on enemies nearing the bloodied threshold to trigger execution bonuses or force defensive retreats.

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- Always announce when any combatant (PC or monster) crosses the 50% HP line: 'The creature is now Bloodied.'
- Use Bloodied as a narrative turning point to change monster tactics from confident to desperate or feral.
