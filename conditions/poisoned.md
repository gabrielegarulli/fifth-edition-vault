# Poisoned
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> A poisoned creature has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Poisoned condition, you experience the following effect.

#### Ability Checks and Attacks Affected

You have Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Disadvantage:** You have Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- Significantly reduces enemy martial accuracy and grapple capability.

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- Protection from Poison and Lesser Restoration readily cure this condition.
