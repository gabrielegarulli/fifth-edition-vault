# Stunned
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> Incapacitated, can't move, speak only falteringly, auto-fails Str and Dex saves, attacks against have advantage.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Stunned condition, you experience the following effects.

#### Incapacitated

You have the Incapacitated condition.

#### Saving Throws Affected

You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.

#### Attacks Affected

Attack rolls against you have Advantage.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Incapacitated:** You are Incapacitated and can't move.
- **Speech:** You can speak only falteringly.
- **Auto-Fail Saves:** You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
- **Attacks Against:** Attack rolls against you have Advantage.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- Auto-fails grapple escapes and Dex saving throws like Disintegrate.

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- Unlike Paralyzed, Stunned does NOT grant automatic critical hits.
