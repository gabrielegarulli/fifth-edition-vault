# Invisible
*Condition | Source: SRD 5.2.1*

> An invisible creature has surprise advantages, cannot be targeted by sight-dependent effects, attacks with advantage, and is immune to Opportunity Attacks.

## 📜 Rule Text

While you have the Invisible condition, you experience the following effects.

#### Surprise

If you're Invisible when you roll Initiative, you have Advantage on the roll.

#### Concealed

You aren't affected by any effect that requires its target to be seen unless the effect's creator can somehow see you. Any equipment you are wearing or carrying is also concealed.

#### Attacks Affected

Attack rolls against you have Disadvantage, and your attack rolls have Advantage. If a creature can somehow see you, you don't gain this benefit against that creature.

## ⚔️ Key Effects & Rules Breakdown

- **Surprise / Initiative:** You have Advantage on Initiative rolls if you are Invisible when rolling.
- **Concealment:** You aren't affected by any effect that requires its target to be seen unless the effect's creator can see you (e.g., through Blindsight, Truesight, or See Invisibility). Any equipment you are wearing or carrying is also concealed.
- **Attacks Affected:** Attack rolls against you have Disadvantage, and your attack rolls have Advantage. If a creature can see through your invisibility, this benefit does not apply against that creature.
- **Immunity to Opportunity Attacks:** Opportunity Attacks cannot be triggered by an Invisible creature moving out of reach, because Opportunity Attacks explicitly require the attacker to see the target.

## 🎯 Tactical Combat Implications

- **Pre-Combat Invisibility:** Casting Invisibility or using a potion prior to kicking in a door effectively gives your team the drop on enemies by ensuring high initiative placement.
- **Free Disengage:** Invisible rogues and spellcasters can reposition around melee frontliners without using their action to Disengage.
- **Shutting Down Enemy Casters:** Most single-target control spells require line of sight. Being Invisible protects your concentration casters from Counterspell and targeted debuffs.

## 🛡️ GM Referee & Adjudication Guidance

- Remember that an invisible creature still makes sound and leaves footprints; enemies know which 5-foot square the creature occupies unless it successfully takes the Hide action.
- Creatures with Blindsight, Truesight, or Tremorsense negate the defensive and offensive attack roll benefits within their sensory range.
