## New

**Import from 5etools, by SainHT**
- Point Settings → 5etools at a 5etools folder or JSON file in your vault, and its spells, monsters, feats and items become notes in your homebrew folder, under 5etools
- A record already in SRD 5.2.1, or one whose note exists, is skipped, and the import says how many notes it wrote and skipped

**Homebrew notes from a form**
- New homebrew spell, feat, item, magic item and condition: a button on the Spells, Feats, Items and Magic Items pages, and a command for each kind
- Fill in the form and see the note as its catalog will show it, or as markdown
- Save writes one new note into your homebrew folder, never over an existing file, and checks it reads back with every field
- A feat's benefits and a magic item's worn effects are picked from a list, so they reach the sheet
- A homebrew spell, feat, item or magic item has Edit beside Open note, which opens it in the same form

**Import a D&D Beyond character from a saved file**
- Import from a file on the Characters page, with steps for saving the file from D&D Beyond. The plugin still contacts no server
- Feats, spells and items your vault doesn't have are listed as notes to create, and converting creates them in your homebrew folder for you to fill in
- Importing the same character again finds its note, and a converted character's card can Re-convert it

**Choices the builder now asks**
- A class's tool choices: a Bard's three instruments, a Monk's artisan's tools or instrument
- A class skill your background already gives is marked, and a tool both give asks for another, as the 2024 rules say
- Add to a character in the Feats catalog asks the feat's choices, such as Magic Initiate's spell list and spells, and can add a repeatable feat again

## Changed

**The sheet applies more of the rules**
- Class features: Second Wind, Wild Shape and Channel Divinity get one use back on a short rest and all on a long rest. Unarmored Defense uses the best formula you qualify for. Martial Arts works with Monk weapons
- Subclasses: the Champion's critical range shows on attack rows, and Remarkable Athlete gives initiative advantage. Elemental Affinity, Empowered Evocation and Disciple of Life add to spells, and Frenzy and Colossus Slayer add their dice
- A subclass that casts spells gets its spell save DC and attack when you level into it, and its own spell list when you add spells
- Feats: Great Weapon Fighting is offered on damage rolls, Savage Attacker on weapon damage only and you keep either roll, and Magic Initiate casts with its own ability, so a Fighter has a spell DC
- Boon of Truesight, Boon of the Night Spirit, Boon of Fate and Grappler apply on the sheet
- Magic weapons and armour use their base item, so a Dagger of Venom has dice and Dragon Scale Mail has an AC, and their stated bonuses count
- Attack rows show the weapon's properties, range, extra damage, its mastery and what it does, and the ammunition you carry
- Armour you aren't trained with, armour's Stealth disadvantage and worn items' own advantages reach the rolls
- A class's tool proficiencies reach the sheet
- "Its range increases by 60 feet" adds to a Darkvision you already have, and homebrew effects can say so

## Fixed

- Clearing a background no longer keeps its ability increases
- The monster template's fire immunity shows in the Bestiary, and the half-caster class template has spell slots at level 1
- A D&D Beyond import keeps the species' lineage, size, spellcasting ability and skill picks
- An item or magic item note using an item effect is no longer reported as a mistake
- The Bard's skills, the class tables and Shillelagh's damage read correctly in the SRD notes
