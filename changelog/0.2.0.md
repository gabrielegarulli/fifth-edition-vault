## New

**Every species choice in the builder**
- A Goliath picks its Giant Ancestry and a Dragonborn its Draconic Ancestry, and the sheet shows the traits that choice gives
- A species that gives a skill, tool, language or weapon of your choice asks for it, and the ones it always gives reach the sheet too
- A Human picks the Origin feat Versatile gives, with its own choices, beside the background's
- An Elf's or Tiefling's lineage now gives what it says: the Drow's 120 ft Darkvision, the Wood Elf's 35 ft speed, each Fiendish Legacy's resistance
- A species trait that starts at a later level appears on the Features tab when you reach it, and a choice made at a later level is asked when you level up

**A tutorial for writing your own rules notes**
- A page for each kind of note: species, classes, subclasses, backgrounds, feats, spells, items, magic items, monsters and conditions
- Each page starts from the smallest note that works, then shows one with every key the plugin reads, and ends with the mistakes that kind invites
- "Effects, the short list" shows how to give a note benefits that land on the sheet
- Read it at github.com/gabrielegarulli/fifth-edition-vault, in docs/homebrew. The README links to it

**Your own rules notes**
- Put homebrew species, classes, subclasses, feats, spells, items and more in one folder, Homebrew by default, and every catalog and the builder read them. Change the folder under Settings → Note folders
- The command "Add homebrew templates" writes a short and a full template for each kind of note into a folder you choose. A subclass that casts spells has its own template
- An open note says what in it the plugin cannot use, such as a misspelled skill or an effect that does not exist, and so do the species and background cards in the builder
- When two notes have the same name, the catalog says which one it shows and which it hides

## Changed

- Characters are read from the character folder and its subfolders, and feats from the feats folder or the homebrew folder. A note outside them is no longer found, so move it into one
- A subclass's spell table reads as a sentence, such as "Choice of 2 Wizard evocation cantrips or level 1–2 spells"

## Fixed

- Resistances, immunities and speeds from items you wear now reach the sheet
- A Barbarian's and a Monk's level 20 feature can raise those scores to 25
