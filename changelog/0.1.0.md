## New

**A new Character Builder**
- The steps come in the order most players know: Class, Background, Species, Abilities, Equipment, Spells, then Review
- One column, with the steps, your character's name and a portrait from your vault along the top
- Only a missing name, class or species stops you saving. Anything else left open is a warning you can come back to
- Review lists what is still to answer, and each line takes you to its step
- Each ability shows how its score adds up. Add a bonus with a reason, or set the score outright
- Write down your character's alignment, looks, personality, ideals, bonds, flaws and backstory on the Background step

**Spells on the Atlas VTT map**
- Cast a spell at a token and the map draws it, with Atlas VTT's own effects (Atlas VTT 0.15.0 or later)
- Click the target's token to aim. Escape cancels

**Update available**
- When a newer release is out, a notice says so, with Update and What's new buttons
- Update opens the plugin's page in Community plugins, where Obsidian installs it
- The check reads one file, the plugin's published manifest on GitHub, once an hour. Switch it off under Settings → Updates

**What's new**
- After an update, this window shows what changed, once, with the releases you haven't seen marked NEW
- Open it again at any time with the command "Show what's new"
- Choose whether it opens after updates, and whether only for feature releases

**Settings search**
- Obsidian's settings search (Obsidian 1.13 and later) now finds this plugin's settings

## Fixed

- Your carrying limit now counts your Constitution modifier
- With only one campaign, rolls, the activity log and encounters use it without you choosing it first
